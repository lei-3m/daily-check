# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Daily Check is for a single user managing a lightweight personal workday across phone and PC. The main context is quick capture, checking, copying, and moving small checklist items without project-management overhead.

## Product Purpose

The product provides a date-based daily checklist, memo space, upcoming schedule view, and synced storage so the same small working set is available across devices. Success means the user can open the app, know today's work, update it quickly, and copy the checklist in Markdown form.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, direct, practical. The interface should feel like a reliable pocket notebook: compact, readable, and low ceremony.

## Anti-references

Avoid project-management complexity, dashboard metrics, decorative marketing layouts, hover-only controls, and anything that makes the checklist feel like a performance score.

## Design Principles

- Keep the checklist central; supporting blocks must not bury today's tasks.
- Visible controls beat hidden interactions because mobile is the primary device.
- Tap visible things and respond in place unless a button clearly means navigation.
- Store device-specific preferences locally and user data in the synced AppState.
- Preserve Markdown checklist copy output regardless of visual checkbox styling.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard focus visibility, touch targets around 44px where practical, readable contrast in light and dark themes, and reduced-motion-safe interactions.
